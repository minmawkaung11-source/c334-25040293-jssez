Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone

Grow across the Causeway

The JS-SEZ links Singapore's innovation ecosystem with Johor's production strengths. It covers over 3,500 km², with nine investment zones across 11 sectors.

  • 9flagship zones
  • 11sectors
  • 3,500+ km²total area

The nine zones

Each zone targets different activities. Choose the one that fits your industry.

  • Senai-Skudai

    Aerospace manufacturing; maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO).

  • Kulai-Sedenak

    AI; quantum technology supply chain; medical devices; pharmaceuticals.

  • Tanjung Pelepas

    Logistics; port operations.

  • Johor Bahru City Centre

    Global services; digital economy.

  • Iskandar Puteri

    Manufacturing; tourism; education.

  • Forest City Special Financial Zone

    Global services; smart logistics; relocation services.

  • Tanjung Langsat-Kong Kong

    Downstream specialty chemicals.

  • Pengerang Integrated Petroleum Complex

    Oil and gas; petrochemicals.

  • Desaru-Penawar

    Integrated tourism.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 9.

Incentives

  • 5% corporate tax for 10 years, or 15 years, for manufacturers with qualifying capital investment (excluding land) of at least RM500 million or RM1 billion.
  • 5% personal income tax for knowledge workers.
  • Sector-specific incentives.
  • Grants and facilitation support from both sides.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 6.

Tax watch-outs

  • Incentive rules differ. Schemes in Singapore and Malaysia have different conditions. Poor structuring can mean missing out (p. 18).
  • Transfer pricing. Price related-party deals at arm's length and keep records in both countries (p. 17).
  • Permanent establishment. A significant presence in Johor may make profits taxable there (p. 17).
  • Staff tax. The JS-SEZ does not override domestic tax rules. Track workdays and locations for staff working in Johor (p. 20).

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 17, 18 and 20.

Passes for staff

  • Employment Pass

    For expatriate professionals in manufacturing, selected services and approved regional offices. Posts are Key Posts or Term Posts, approved by MIDA. Apply through Xpats Gateway.

    pp. 31–32

  • Professional Visit Pass

    For staff of an overseas company doing short-term work, such as training, consultancy or machine installation. Up to 12 months and not extendable.

    p. 32

  • Investor Pass

    A multi-entry facility for new, pipeline and existing investors, such as founders, directors and senior decision-makers. Up to 12 months, in 6-month periods.

    p. 33

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 31–33.
